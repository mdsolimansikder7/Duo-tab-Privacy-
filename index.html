DuoTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

DuoTab is a browser extension that lets you use multiple accounts on the same website at the same time by giving each session tab its own separate cookies.

Data DuoTab handles

To keep each session tab’s login separate, DuoTab reads and stores:

  • Cookies set by websites you visit inside a session tab. These can include login (session) cookies.
  • Which sites are open in each session tab, so you can reopen closed sessions.
  • Your session settings, such as each tab’s number, color and name.

DuoTab does not collect your name, email address, passwords, location, or the content of the pages you visit. It does not record clicks, scrolling or keystrokes.

Where data is stored

All of this data stays on your device, in the browser’s extension storage. DuoTab does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. There are no servers, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no usage tracking.

Export and import

If you use Export JSON, your sessions are saved to a file on your device. That file may contain login cookies, so keep it private. Anyone who has the file may be able to access the accounts it contains.

Permissions

  • tabs: to identify, open and close session tabs.
  • storage: to keep sessions and their cookies on your device.
  • webRequest: to read cookies from responses in session tabs and save them locally.
  • declarativeNetRequest: to send only the matching session’s cookies in session tabs and block the browser’s regular cookies there.
  • Access to all sites: so you can use separate logins on any website you choose.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data is used only for DuoTab’s single purpose: keeping separate logins in separate tabs.

Removing your data

You can close a session to remove its data, or uninstall DuoTab to remove everything the extension has stored.
